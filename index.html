The idea

A calmer frame for connected living.

Flow’s Android prototype brings a range of product ideas under one visual language. The focus is clarity: familiar navigation, grounded color, and space for people to find what matters to them.

Everyday tools

The platform

One identity, across screens.

The Flow experience is being developed across mobile and web. Here is what each platform currently contains in the project.

Android · Prototype

The fullest interface today.

A multi-screen Compose app with local sample content, settings, and a device-local app gate. Its service screens are demonstrations.

iOS · Preview

A native introduction.

A SwiftUI welcome experience that introduces the Flow identity. Account, data, and service integrations are not present.

Web · Preview

A focused marketplace flow.

The web app can browse published listings and save private drafts when configured with Supabase and the API. It has no ordering or payment flow.

i

Clear about what is real. There is no production payment, transfer, identity-verification, messaging, or AI service in this preview. Sample balances, profiles, ratings, and activity are not verified information. The web preview’s account and listing functions require services configured by an operator.
