The idea

A calmer frame for connected living.

Flow brings a range of product ideas under one visual language. The focus is clarity: familiar navigation, grounded color, and space for people to find what matters to them.

↗

Community

Explore local social and discovery ideas in the Android app. Example people and activity are local demonstration content.

⌂

Local commerce

Browse published listings in the web app when configured. Android marketplace and directory screens currently use local examples.

◌

Everyday tools

Messaging and wallet screens in Android use local demonstration data. They do not send messages or move money.

The platform

One identity, across screens.

Flow keeps a familiar identity across mobile and web. Here is what each platform currently offers.

Android · Mobile app

The fullest interface today.

A multi-screen Compose app with settings and a device-local app gate. Community, messaging, marketplace, and wallet examples use local data.

iOS · Mobile app

A native introduction.

A SwiftUI welcome experience for Flow. Account, shared data, and service integrations are not available yet.

Web · Browser app

Use Flow from a browser.

Create an account, manage your profile, save private listing drafts, submit reports, and discover published listings when Firebase is configured.

i

Clear about what is live. Payments, transfers, identity verification, messaging, and AI services are not available. Android example balances, profiles, ratings, and activity are local demonstration data, not verified information. Browser account and listing tools require configured Firebase services; listings remain private drafts unless published by an authorized service. Saved report records stay private and do not reach a support team.
