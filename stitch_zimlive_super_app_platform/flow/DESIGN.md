## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-velocity, fluid digital ecosystem transitioning from an informational broadcast medium into an interconnected lifestyle and financial super-app. The product blends transactional precision with organic social vitality, uniting real-time messaging, social interaction, cross-border digital banking, local commerce directories, and intelligent automation into an effortless continuum.

### Aesthetic Movement
The visual language merges **Modern Crisp Fintech** with **Fluid Kinetic Minimalism**:
- **Controlled Fluidity:** Micro-interactions and spatial containers mimic natural hydraulic motion—supple transitions, subtle pill badges, and generous corner radiuses paired with razor-sharp data tables and ledger typography.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts favor clear editorial zoning over cluttered dashboards, utilizing pristine white space, deep contrast hierarchies, and luminous emerald accents to guide intent without sensory exhaustion.
- **Atmospheric Vitality:** Soft structural blurs and crisp boundary lines delineate financial security and conversational intimacy, evoking reliability, optimism, and continuous momentum.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Crafted for an ambitious, mobile-first international demographic managing multi-currency cash flows, entrepreneurial endeavors, and fast-paced community communication. The interface should invoke:
- **Instantaneous Trust:** Solid, authoritative dark evergreen foundational surfaces that anchor monetary transactions.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Luminous mint and electric emerald indicators signaling active connectivity, liquid assets, and real-time computation.
- **Frictionless Motion:** Components that feel light under the finger and responsive to rapid contextual pivots.

## Colors

The palette balances the gravitas of institutional asset custody with the luminous clarity of modern mobile software.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0B3B2F` - Deep Forest Slate):** Represents stability, authoritative infrastructure, primary buttons, high-level headers, and high-contrast dark states.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Vibrant Emerald Flow):** The engine of interaction—action triggers, active tab indicators, completed financial state pills, wallet gains, and live conversation beacons.
- **Tertiary (`#064E3B` - Deep Pine):** Secondary structural accents, focused interactive borders, selected chips, and pressed micro-states.
- **Neutral Surface System (`#F4F7F5` - Mint Wash Neutral):** Foundations diverge from sterile grays into an organic, cool-tinted atmosphere. Complementary tokens include `#FFFFFF` (Base Surface), `#E6FBF2` (Mint Tinted Surface/Success Fill), `#E2E8E5` (Neutral Border), and `#0F1715` (Deep Slate Text).
- **Signal Accents:** High-voltage amber (`#F59E0B`) for pending Escrow or dynamic validation; bright coral red (`#EF4444`) strictly reserved for destructive authorizations and balance alerts.

### Color Rules & Usage
- **The 70-20-10 Distribution:** 70% pure canvas and soft neutral surfaces (`#FFFFFF` to `#F4F7F5`), 20% authoritative forest slate structural elements and text (`#0B3B2F`), and 10% electric emerald (`#10B981`) reserved strictly for forward momentum—CTA buttons, positive balances, active AI status indicators, and notification pips.
- **Mint Enclosures:** Reserve `#E6FBF2` strictly for contextual containment—such as incoming payment highlights, verified merchant trust tags, and FlowAI conversational response cards.
- **Accessibility:** Ensure any text resting on `#10B981` is white (`#FFFFFF`) or deepest forest slate (`#0B3B2F`) to satisfy strict WCAG AAA guidelines. Secondary text must not drop below a 4.5:1 contrast against neutral backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy intentionally contrasts structural distinction with operational legibility.

### Font Pairing Strategy
- **Display & Headings (Syne):** Syne delivers distinct, sculptured letterforms that command attention and provide a progressive, premium fintech brand signature. Used across screen headers, financial balances, merchant business names, and marketing landing zones.
- **Interface & Operational Text (Inter):** Inter provides neutral clarity across messaging threads, ledger line items, forms, metadata tags, and conversational FlowAI outputs.

### Typographic Directives
- **Financial Balance Legibility:** Multi-currency balance figures utilize Syne (`headline-xl`) with tabular figures (`tnum`) enabled via open-type features to eliminate layout shifting during real-time balance fluctuations.
- **Negative Tracking:** All Syne headings larger than 24px enforce negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to produce a compressed, bespoke editorial quality.
- **Label Formatting:** All `label-sm` elements (transaction tags, directory verification status, online presence) must use uppercase formatting with widened letter spacing (`0.04em`) to maintain micro-scale optical legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes an adaptive fluid grid structured around strict vertical and horizontal rhythm, ensuring effortless transitions between high-density financial ledgers and spacious social feeds.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` section margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum application canvas capped at 1440px for desktop browser stability.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid, `2rem` margins, `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Bottom navigation anchoring replaces top and lateral navigation structures.

### Layout Principles
- **Docked Action Zones:** On mobile clients, bottom action bars and transaction submission modules float `1rem` above the bottom screen boundary within floating pill containers.
- **Dual-Pane Workspaces:** On desktop and tablet viewports, chat listings and financial ledger details employ a 4:8 split pane (4 columns for navigational list/directory, 8 columns for active conversation thread, FlowAI workspace, or detailed transaction telemetry).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Stacking**, **Glassmorphic Boundaries**, and **Ambient Luminous Shadows**.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (Ground 0):** Base canvas background (`#F4F7F5`). Completely matte, defining the atmospheric substrate.
2. **Surface Level (Level 1):** Solid white containers (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro-thin outline: `1px solid rgba(11, 59, 47, 0.06)`. Used for directory cards, feed posts, and conversation panels.
3. **Floating Elevators (Level 2):** Quick-transfer trays, modal panels, and context menus. Elevated using an ambient emerald-tinted drop shadow:
   `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 59, 47, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.04)`.
4. **Overlay / Glassmorphic Tiers (Level 3):** Floating header navbars, audio bars, and dynamic wallet status overlays use semi-translucent backdrops (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`) with a `16px` backdrop-blur filter and an edge highlight border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).

### Atmospheric Glow
Interactive call-to-actions, primary wallet action buttons, and active FlowAI thought states introduce a concentrated ambient glow:
`0 8px 24px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances human warmth with enterprise-grade utility through rounded rectangles and pure pill geometry.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Base Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 1rem & `rounded-xl` / 1.5rem):** Standard feed items, balance cards, and directory shopfront panels adopt `1.5rem` (24px) corner radii. This creates the signature modern, tactile container feel.
- **Interactive Controls & Status Badges (Pills / 9999px):** Primary/secondary buttons, chip filters, search input pills, and notification indicators adopt continuous, fully rounded pill boundaries (`9999px`).
- **Inner Nested Alignment:** Nested elements within containers strictly respect optical corner concentricity: an element with `1.5rem` outer padding holding a secondary item requires an inner radius of `0.75rem` to prevent visual awkwardness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill shape, `#0B3B2F` fill, pure white text, Syne weight 600 or Inter label-lg. Hover shifts to `#064E3B` with a subtle elevation shift. Focus states display an electric emerald (`#10B981`) offset ring (2px width, 2px offset).
- **Secondary (Kinetic Accent):** Full pill shape, vibrant `#10B981` background, `#0B3B2F` text for punchy contrast, accompanied by an ambient green drop shadow.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent fill, `1px solid rgba(11, 59, 47, 0.15)`, text color `#0B3B2F`. Hover fills with `#E6FBF2`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact pill dimensions (`space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal padding).
- **Active State:** `#E6FBF2` fill, `#0B3B2F` or `#064E3B` text, accented with a 6px pulsing `#10B981` circular status dot.
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive chips sit in white with subtle border. Selected chips fill `#0B3B2F` with white typography.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Fully rounded pill contours (`9999px`) for contextual global search bars; `rounded-lg` (0.75rem) for complex multi-line transactional inputs.
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid rgba(11, 59, 47, 0.12)`.
- Focused: `1px solid #10B981`, paired with an ambient ring `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.16)`.
- Labels float top-left in `label-sm` uppercase forest slate.

### Cards & Feed Tiles
- Structural boundary with `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) outer radius.
- Standard background is pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` crisp border in `rgba(11, 59, 47, 0.05)`.
- Cards never use heavy dark borders; division is achieved via clean negative space and surface tint variations.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular check architecture across both single and multi-select components to maintain fluid consistency.
- Selected state fills with `#10B981`, displaying a crisp white micro-check icon.

### Super-Wallet Card (Specialized Component)
- Distinct gradient overlay: deep forest slate (`#0B3B2F`) moving diagonally to deep pine (`#064E3B`) with ambient refractive emerald lighting.
- Currency balance set in Syne `headline-xl` (mobile: `headline-xl-mobile`) in crisp white.
- Embedded transfer/request buttons styled as frosted glass pill buttons (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).

### FlowAI Assistant Node (Specialized Component)
- Dynamic conversational bubble styled with `#E6FBF2` background and `#0B3B2F` typography.
- Border framed with a delicate, animated gradient border transitioning between `#10B981` and `#E6FBF2`.
- Active assistant indicator: Luminous, multi-layered pulsing emerald beacon.
